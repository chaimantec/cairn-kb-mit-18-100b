## What comes from the course, and on what terms

- `raw/transcripts/` is adapted from OCW's published caption files for the lecture videos. `raw/transcripts/original/` holds the caption text regrouped into timestamped paragraphs. The files directly in `raw/transcripts/` add a light copy-edit, with every uncertain change marked.
- `raw/notes/` is a page-by-page transcription of the course's typed lecture notes (PDF), by Tobias Holck Colding, with the mathematics set in LaTeX.

These are adaptations of CC BY-NC-SA material and carry the same licence. Attribution: *MIT 18.100B Real Analysis, Spring 2025, MIT OpenCourseWare, <https://ocw.mit.edu>, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.* OCW's citation guidance and terms of use are at <https://ocw.mit.edu/terms>.

The wiki prose in `wiki/` is original writing that summarizes and cites the material above. It is offered under the same licence.

## Not in this repository

- **PDFs.** No course PDF is committed. [`sources.md`](sources.md) links each one at its canonical OCW URL.
- **Images.** None. The Lecture 1 notes contain no figures.
- **The textbook.** Thomson, Bruckner and Bruckner, *Elementary Real Analysis*, is linked, not copied. Its own site offers it as a free download.
